About

I build variational autoencoders with binary latent spaces and use error-correcting codes to make their inference sharper.

I am a PhD student in Machine Learning for Precision Medicine at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, advised by Prof. Pablo M. Olmos. I apply coded binary bottlenecks to single-cell generative models such as scVI and MrVI, aiming for latent codes whose individual bits line up with cell lineages, states and tissue niches.

Before Madrid, I earned an MSc in Bioinformatics at the University of Copenhagen, working with Prof. Anders Krogh on generative decoders for incomplete multi-modal clinical data. My first degree was in Pharmaceutical Engineering.

Research

Discrete-latent VAEs with error-correcting codes 2026 – now Doctoral research, UC3M · with Pablo M. Olmos
  • Coded VAEs replace continuous latents with binary bottlenecks protected by error-correcting codes, with a derivation of why they pay less KL at matched decoder capacity.
  • Binarized the latent space of scVI and MrVI and packaged the method as a standalone scvi-tools plugin, leaving the encoder and decoder untouched.
  • Model-agnostic evaluation on PBMC, Haniffa COVID-19 and Heart Cell Atlas: clustering agreement, per-bit AUROC, differential expression, and out-of-distribution uncertainty.
Stochastic training for generative decoders on incomplete multi-modal data 2025 MSc thesis, Center for Health Data Science, University of Copenhagen · with Anders Krogh
  • Three feature-level stochastic training strategies for deep generative decoders on clinical, multi-omics and imaging data, improving prediction by about 5%.
GWAS of type 2 diabetes variants in the Greenlandic population 2024 Project researcher, University of Copenhagen · with Anders Albrechtsen
  • Longitudinal and cross-sectional association analyses across five cohorts (~6,000 individuals) with linear mixed models and population-structure correction.
